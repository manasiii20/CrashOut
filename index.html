CrashOut

Welcome to CrashOut! Your mission is to make sure the screen is clear of the blocks.

Controls

  • Move your avatar with the mouse
  • Push blocks to clear them off screen

Objectives

  • Clear all blocks before time runs out
  • Survive as long as possible

Challenges

  • Watch out for red blocks
  • They will actively chase you!

Pro Tip

Use quick mouse movements to push blocks more forcefully!
